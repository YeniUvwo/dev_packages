THE PACKAGE

What’s included

  • Custom online store design, responsive on mobile, tablet and desktop.
  • Up to 5 standard website pages, plus the agreed store pages and system screens.
  • Product catalogue with no fixed product-count limit within the agreed system.
  • Admin dashboard to add, edit and delete products after launch.
  • Product images, descriptions, prices and stock/status fields.
  • Shopping cart and checkout.
  • Integration with one agreed payment gateway, subject to provider approval and availability.
  • Order management dashboard to view and update order status.
  • Customer order confirmation emails where supported by the agreed setup.
  • Basic on-page SEO, testing, launch support and 3 rounds of revisions.

CLEAR SCOPE

What’s not included

  • Payment gateway transaction charges, bank charges or payment-provider fees.
  • Domain name, hosting, paid themes/plugins or third-party service fees.
  • Delivery company fees, courier accounts or advanced shipping integrations.
  • Bulk product data entry, product photography or writing product descriptions; you can add and manage products yourself after launch.
  • Advanced product variations, subscriptions, multi-vendor selling, loyalty programmes or complex inventory/warehouse systems unless separately agreed.
  • Accounting, ERP, POS, CRM or other third-party integrations unless included in the agreed scope.
  • Ongoing maintenance, support or new features after launch.
Product limit: There is no fixed number of products built into the store. You can add and manage your catalogue yourself. The package covers the agreed product-management system; advanced catalogue features or unusually complex integrations may cost extra.

BEFORE WE BEGIN

Package details

  • We’ll agree the payment gateway, order statuses, delivery approach and checkout requirements before development.
  • You provide product details, images, prices, policies and business information unless separately agreed.
  • Payment gateway accounts and approval must be completed by you; third-party providers may have their own requirements.
  • Revisions cover the agreed design and store flow—not new integrations or a new design direction.
  • Domain, hosting, transaction charges and third-party fees are separate from the package price.
  • Final price and delivery timeline are confirmed after the requirements are agreed.
