THE PACKAGE

What’s included

  • Up to 8 website pages.
  • Custom design aligned with your brand.

CLEAR SCOPE

What’s not included

  • Online store, cart, checkout or payment gateway.
  • Booking system, membership area or custom web application.
  • Drag-and-drop page builder or changing the site’s layout, colours, fonts or navigation from the dashboard.
  • Editing general website page text, images, banners or sections through the dashboard; these changes are handled separately.
  • Domain, hosting, paid themes/plugins or third-party service fees.
  • Copywriting, professional photography, logo design or bulk data entry.
  • Ongoing updates and maintenance after launch.

BEFORE WE BEGIN

  • You provide final text, images, logo and business information unless separately agreed.
  • Revisions cover the agreed design and content, not new features or a new design direction.
  • Domain, hosting and third-party fees are separate from the package price.
  • Final price and delivery timeline are confirmed after the requirements are agreed.
