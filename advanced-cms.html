THE PACKAGE

What’s included

  • Up to 10 website pages, depending on the agreed scope.
  • Custom website design and responsive layouts for mobile, tablet and desktop.
  • Secure admin dashboard with login access.
  • Edit agreed page headings, text and images.
  • Add, edit and delete services or other agreed repeatable content items.
  • Create, edit and delete blog posts, images and categories where required.
  • Manage agreed website sections and selected business information.
  • Contact form with email notifications, WhatsApp/social links and basic on-page SEO.
  • Website testing, launch support and 3 rounds of revisions.

CLEAR SCOPE

What’s not included

  • E-commerce, cart, checkout or payment gateway unless separately agreed.
  • A drag-and-drop page builder or full visual redesign tools inside the dashboard.
  • Complex custom applications, booking systems, membership portals or third-party integrations unless included in the written scope.
  • Unlimited page layouts or unlimited custom features.
  • Domain, hosting, paid themes/plugins or third-party service fees.
  • Copywriting, professional photography, logo design or bulk data entry.
  • Ongoing maintenance and new features after launch.
Important: The dashboard is designed for the content types and fields agreed before work begins. New modules or functionality outside that scope are quoted separately.

BEFORE WE BEGIN

Package details

  • We will agree which pages and content areas are editable before development starts.
  • Editing content does not include changing the site’s layout, fonts, colours, navigation or underlying functionality.
  • You provide final text, images, logo and business information unless separately agreed.
  • Revisions cover the agreed design and features—not new modules or a new design direction.
  • Domain, hosting and third-party fees are separate from the package price.
  • Final price and delivery timeline are confirmed after requirements are documented.
